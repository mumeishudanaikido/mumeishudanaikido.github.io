Our Training Culture

Flow, connection, and progressive learning in a welcoming environment

Ki Musubi: The Art of Connection

Our training centers on Ki Musubi—"knotting Ki"—the art of matching your partner's movement and intention from its inception. We emphasize flow and fluidity over brute force, developing clear, flexible, and attentive minds.

  • Train with partners of all levels in mixed-ability classes
  • Learn through progressive sequences built week by week
  • Develop connection and awareness through Ki Musubi
  • Experience personalized skill development at every level

Our Philosophy

Our dojo follows the teachings of O-Sensei Morihei Ueshiba through Aikikai Foundation guidance. We maintain traditional values while making Aikido accessible to modern practitioners.

Under Serge Beraud Sensei (6th Dan), we emphasize:

  • Respect and humility in training
  • Continuous personal development
  • Integration of body, mind, and spirit
  • Community and mutual support
