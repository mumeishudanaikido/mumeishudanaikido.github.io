Our Philosophy

Our dojo follows the teachings of O-Sensei Morihei Ueshiba, the founder of Aikido, through the guidance of the Aikikai Foundation. We maintain traditional values while making the art accessible to modern practitioners.

Under the guidance of Serge Beraud Sensei (6th Dan), our practice emphasizes:

  • Respect and humility in training
  • Continuous personal development
  • The integration of body, mind, and spirit
  • Community and mutual support
