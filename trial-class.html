Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be in shape to start?

No! Aikido training will help you get in shape. We welcome students of all fitness levels and will work with you at your own pace.

Is Aikido effective for self-defense?

Yes. Aikido teaches practical techniques for neutralizing attacks while minimizing harm. However, it also emphasizes awareness and conflict avoidance as the first line of defense.

How long does it take to earn a black belt?

Typically 4-6 years of consistent training. However, Aikido is a lifelong journey, and rank is less important than personal growth and understanding.

What's the training schedule?

We offer classes throughout the week for different age groups and skill levels. Check our schedule page for details.

How much does it cost?

Membership fees vary by age and commitment level. We'll discuss pricing options when you visit. Your first class is always free.

Can I just watch a class first?

Absolutely! You're welcome to observe a class before trying it yourself. However, we find most people prefer to participate and experience Aikido firsthand.
