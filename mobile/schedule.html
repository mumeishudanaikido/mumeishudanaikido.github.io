Nanyang Technological University - NTU Aikido Club

The Wave @ NTU Sports and Recreation Centre (SRC) Activity Room 3, 10 Nanyang Crescent, Singapore 636956

NTU Aikido Club began in 1998 and officially launched training in 1999. After its inception to Mumei Shudan in 2010, NTU Aikido Club has grown maturely and has fostered a supportive, technically solid environment where multiple members have achieved black belt (Dan) ranks. Along the way, it has engaged with other Aikido clubs across tertiary institutions to enrich its practices through its inaugural Inter-University Aikido Seminar (which was spearheaded by NTU Aikido Club in 2015).

National University of Singapore - NUS Aikido Club

Location 1: Multi Purpose Sports Hall (MPSH2) Sports & Recreation Centre, National University Of Singapore (NUS), 2 Sports Drive 1, Singapore 117561

Location 2: Stephen Riady Centre, National University Of Singapore (NUS), 2 College Ave W, Singapore 138607

NUS Aikido, established in 2014, has since evolved into a thriving martial arts community, forging a key partnership with Mumei Shudan in 2021. This collaboration has significantly enhanced the club's training, providing members with a high-quality learning environment rooted in the principles of traditional Aikido. As a martial art that emphasizes harmony and non-violent conflict resolution, Aikido offers a unique path for both physical and mental development. The core mission of NUS Aikido is to build a welcoming and inclusive community. The club is a place where students, alumni, and staff from all backgrounds can unite through a shared passion for learning and self-improvement. It's more than just a training space; it's a supportive network where individuals can destress from academic and professional pressures while engaging in a fulfilling physical activity. The club strives to create a fun and engaging atmosphere, both on and off the mats, through activities like social gatherings and team events.

Training Sessions

  • Fridays: 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Choa Chu Kang Sports Centre (ActiveSG)

1 Choa Chu Kang St 53, Singapore 689236

Since 2001, Choa Chu Kang Sport Centre, formerly the Choa Chu Kang Sport and Recreation Centre, has been a vibrant hub of activity, offering a wide range of integrated facilities that excite all ages. In 2022, the centre's offerings expanded with the introduction of Mumei Shudan Aikido programmes. These classes, catering to a diverse demographic from youth to seniors, have brought a new dimension of martial arts and holistic wellness to the community. This partnership has significantly enriched the centre's mission, making it a cornerstone for recreation, personal growth, and intergenerational connection in the neighborhood.
